# BOM notes

- Costs are USD estimates for the TRL 3 paper design (SDL-CAL-001 section 11), not quotes. Supplier types are named for each line; no supplier has been chosen.
- Item numbers 1 to 14 match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`, the parts in `cad/src/model.py` and the component table in `docs/02-concept.md`. Item 15 has no callout.
- Item 6 is a set of six plates at about $1.25 each, assuming a printer is available. Plates weigh 40 to 54 g and print in 1.5 to 2.0 h each. Printer time and ownership are not costed.
- Item 2 carries the decided ratio change (SDL-DDR-001 item 4): three wheel sprockets (12, 15 and 18 T), one 15 T plate sprocket, chain for 74 to 77 links with an offset link, and a spring idler.
- Item 12 is the decided bolted frame (SDL-DDR-001 item 8); the rails are 25 x 25 x 1.5 mm tube, sized in SDL-CAL-001 section 7.
- Base seeder (items 1 to 13 and 15): $213.50, which misses the about $200 replication target (SDL-REQ-001 R17). With the row marker kit (item 14): $237.50, inside the $400 prototype budget in `project.yaml`.
- Mass (SDL-CAL-001 section 5): 14.6 kg base, 15.6 kg with the marker kit.
